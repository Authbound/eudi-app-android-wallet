## Full Description (up to 4000 chars)

Experience the future of digital identity with the **Authbound Wallet**. Built upon the official EU Digital Identity (EUDI) Reference Wallet and fully aligned with the eIDAS 2.0 regulation, the Authbound Wallet empowers you to securely manage and share your digital credentials across Europe.

Whether you are a citizen taking control of your personal data or a developer evaluating EUDI-compliant solutions, Authbound provides a seamless, privacy-first experience.

### 🛡️ TAKE CONTROL OF YOUR IDENTITY
• **Secure Credential Storage:** Safely store your Personal Identification Data (PID), Mobile Driving License (mDL), diplomas, and other verifiable credentials directly on your device.
• **Selective Disclosure:** You are in control. Share only the specific information required (like proving you are over 18 without revealing your exact date of birth or full name).
• **Passwordless Authentication:** Instantly and securely log in to public and private digital services without the need for vulnerable passwords.
• **Proximity & Remote Sharing:** Present your credentials online for seamless web access, or in-person using secure QR codes and NFC (ISO 18013-5).
• **Privacy by Design:** Your credentials remain encrypted and local to your device. We do not track your activity or store your sensitive credentials on our servers.

### 🇪🇺 BUILT FOR THE EUDI ECOSYSTEM
As a pioneer in the European Digital Identity space, Authbound ensures full interoperability with the EUDI Architecture and Reference Framework (ARF). Our wallet supports industry-leading standards to ensure future-proof compliance:
• W3C Verifiable Credentials & IETF SD-JWT
• OpenID for Verifiable Credential Issuance (OpenID4VCI)
• OpenID for Verifiable Presentations (OpenID4VP)

### ⚙️ FOR DEVELOPERS & INTEGRATORS
The Authbound Wallet is the perfect companion to the Authbound B2B SaaS platform. Integrators and evaluators can use this app to test instant KYC, credential issuance, and compliant identity verification flows in real-time.

### 📱 HOW IT WORKS
**1. Setup your wallet:** Create your secure account with your email. Hardware-backed cryptographic keys are generated locally on your device to protect your identity.
**2. Add credentials:** Receive verifiable digital credentials from Authbound or supported EUDI ecosystem partners. 
**3. Prove your identity:** Scan a QR code or tap your phone to securely authenticate to services, explicitly consenting to exactly what data is shared.
